Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

IQHerb respects the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect data associated with the use of our application (APIApp) when interacting with Meta (Facebook) platforms, including publishing content, reading engagement data, and replying to comments on our official pages.

1. Information We Collect

Our application interacts with the Facebook Graph API to access the following data related to the Facebook Pages we manage:

  • Basic Page information (name, ID, category)
  • Posts published on the Page and their content
  • Engagement data on posts (likes, reactions, shares)
  • Comments on posts and replies to them
  • Account information of the administrator managing the Page (primary contact email)

2. How We Use This Information

  • Publishing new content to our Pages on our behalf
  • Retrieving existing posts for internal display or analysis
  • Reading and analyzing engagement to understand content performance
  • Replying to audience comments on our posts to improve customer service

We do not use this data for any purpose beyond operating our Pages and serving our customers.

3. Sharing Information with Third Parties

No data collected through this application is shared with any third party for marketing or commercial purposes. The data is used solely internally within IQHerb's systems to operate our Facebook Pages.

4. Data Storage and Security

Access tokens and any related data are stored securely and are not shared or publicly disclosed. We take reasonable measures to protect this data from unauthorized access.

5. Data Deletion

Any user or Page owner who wishes to disconnect the application or delete their associated data may contact us via the email address below, and we will process the request and delete the relevant data within a reasonable period.

6. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, along with an updated "Last updated" date above.
